# BusinessOS Gate G

Gate G connects sales conversion, engagement terms, instalments, payment
clearance, legal work, submission and management income assurance.

## Enforced controls

- one tenant-bound commercial engagement per matter;
- professional fees separated from government/disbursement fees;
- instalments must reconcile exactly to the professional fee;
- legal-work activation blocked until the required initial payment clears;
- submission blocked until the configured clearance threshold is collected;
- critical overrides require AAL2, restricted permission, a substantial reason
  and a maximum 30-day expiry;
- immutable audit evidence for engagement creation and gate overrides;
- role-aware lifecycle queue for sales, solicitors, case management, finance,
  compliance and organisation leadership;
- exception registers allow unforeseen scenarios to be owned and escalated.

Gate G does not claim that every business exception can be predicted. Novel
exceptions must enter the controlled exception workflow rather than bypassing
payment, legal or audit controls.
